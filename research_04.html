Radiation-Convection Interactions in Tropical Cyclones

Overview: To examine the role of radiation-convection interactions in the development of Tropical Cyclones (TCs), mechanism denial experiments are conducted using a general circulation model.

Radiative Feedbacks Associated with the MJO

This study uses radiative kernels to diagnose radiative feedbacks associated with the MJO. As an important driver of tropical intraseasonal variability, the MJO can be viewed as a kind of convective aggregation which involves interactions between clouds, circulation, radiation, and water vapor.
