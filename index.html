About Me

I am a researcher dedicated to understanding the complexities of our climate system. My work primarily focuses on using observations and general circulation models to investigate the coupling between clouds, radiation, circulation, and convection.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. at the University of Miami under the guidance of Brian J. Soden, where I studied these interactions in depth.
